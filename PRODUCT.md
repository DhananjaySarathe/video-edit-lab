# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript and Vite. Static local build.

## Users

Creators and editors who have footage and want to describe the edit they have in mind to an AI editor.

## Product Purpose

See an example of a technique, copy its instruction, or collect and reorder several techniques into one editing brief.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The library has format-specific recipes for Reels/Shorts and Long Form, along with technique demonstrations. Creators can browse by edit stage, compare workflow starters, and add individual recipes to the same prompt. Selections, timing and order are stored locally in the browser. Preview footage is licensed stock and original visual assets.

## Brand Commitments

Inherit the Research Lab visual theme from the user's existing Video Edit workspace. Use Newsreader for editorial headings, DM Sans for the interface, and forest green for actions and selection.
