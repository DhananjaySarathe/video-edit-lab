From raw take to a finished edit plan

For Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and YouTube videos. Four steps on the site, then one prompt for your AI.

Before you start

Film your take, and keep it raw

The prompt tells the AI to cut warm-ups, restarts and filler, so there’s no need to film it again for them.

Export the transcript as .srt

CapCut, Premiere, Descript and Whisper all export one. No take yet? Skip it and you get a script to film.

On the site
1

Pick your video

Tap Build my prompt. Choose Reels/Shorts or Long Form, then the kind of video. Each has a beat-by-beat plan.

2

Paste your take

Say what it’s about and paste the transcript. The site finds your three strongest opening lines. Tap Open on this on one.

3

Choose a hook

Fits your line marks the best match. A hook adds the on-screen text, first frame and edit move. Or skip it.

4

Copy the prompt

Pick where it’s going: Claude or ChatGPT, CapCut, Premiere or Resolve, Descript, or a code agent. Then Copy prompt.

Then, in your AI
5

Paste it in

Claude or ChatGPT returns a timed plan for any editor. CapCut, Premiere and Descript get steps in that app. A code agent gets a JSON timeline, so send it your clips.

6

Check it before you export

The prompt ends with a checklist for this kind of video. Ask the AI to check its plan against it, then watch the cut once with the sound off.

One take, two videos

Filmed a YouTube video? Copy its prompt, then tap Make a Reel from this take. The transcript comes along, and the AI is asked for one strong stretch instead of the whole take squeezed down.

Make a Reel from this take

Reels/Shorts run 15–60 s. Your line starts in the first second, and the text has to work with the sound off.

Long Form runs 8 min to an hour. The opening gets about 10 s, re-hooks carry the middle, an end screen closes it.

Want to see a hook or move first? The Hooks and Edit moves shelves play each one on real footage. Your edit, top right, keeps your picks, plus the length, platform and clip names.
